# Release Notes: 29 October 2026

## 1. tyche Founded: Probability, the Description of Samples, Regression, Time Series, and Gaussian Conditioning

In this update tyche, the infrastructure's foundation for chance, is published with its first five modules ([decision 0003](../decisions/0003-the-first-modules.md), [decision 0004](../decisions/0004-gaussian-conditioning.md)).

- **Probability.** Eight distributions as objects (normal, Student, χ², F, gamma, beta, Poisson, binomial), with tails computed directly, and p-values.
- **The description of samples.** Moments with compensated sums, the nine sample quantiles of Hyndman and Fan, weighted statistics, covariance and correlation matrices, held to NIST's certified values.
- **Regression.** Least squares by pivoted QR with classical and robust covariances (HC0–HC3, clustered) and Wald tests; generalised linear models by IRLS, for gaussian, binomial and Poisson responses, with offsets.
- **Time series.** Autocorrelation and the Ljung–Box test; AR and ARIMA models with forecasts; unit-root tests (ADF with MacKinnon's p-values, KPSS) and Engle–Granger cointegration; the Hodrick–Prescott filter; vector autoregressions with order selection, stability, impulse responses and forecasts.
- **Gaussian conditioning.** The posterior of a Gaussian field given noisy readings of it, updated one reading at a time, with the whitened innovations that test the model against its own data. A rover mapping the Moon's crustal field in Cosmopoiesis is its first user.

Three of the Hodrick–Prescott filter's checks miss their limit of 10⁻¹² for now: its cycle sums to zero to 6 × 10⁻¹² and is orthogonal to time to 4 × 10⁻¹¹, and its trend agrees with statsmodels to 5 × 10⁻¹².
