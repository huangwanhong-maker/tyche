# 2026-11-04

## Decisions Under Partial Observation, and Markov Chains Seen Now and Then

In this update tyche plans under uncertainty and fits chains seen at irregular times ([decision 0009](../decisions/0009-decisions-under-partial-observation.md), [decision 0010](../decisions/0010-markov-chains-observed-at-discrete-times.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0071](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0071-agents-and-decisions-for-urban-planning.md)).

- **POMDPs, answered with intervals.** A new module, `decision`, holds:
  - finite POMDPs and Bayes's rule for their beliefs;
  - bounds from the states alone;
  - exact value iteration by incremental pruning;
  - the point-based solvers PBVI, Perseus and HSVI.

  HSVI closes an interval about the optimal value; on the tiger problem at discount 0.75 it contains the published 1.933439.
- **Planning online.** POMCP plans by Monte Carlo tree search on any simulator, its belief kept as particles. A leaf value can replace its rollouts, which on the tiger were too noisy to learn from.
- **Structure and budgets.**
  - Mixed observability.
  - Many assets joined by a budget: an upper bound on what any plan within it can reach, and a ranked plan that keeps it.
  - Costs with budgets, by Lagrangian duality: the constrained policy is a finite-state controller whose spending is computed exactly and agrees with its simulation.
- **Several decision makers.** Dec-POMDPs with exact best responses and JESP. Dec-Tiger's published optima are reproduced: −4.000000 at horizon 2 and 5.190812 at horizon 3.
- **Chains seen now and then.** A new module, `processes`, fits a continuous-time Markov chain to states seen at irregular intervals: the panel likelihood (Kalbfleisch and Lawless), by uniformisation, with a rating's misreading if wanted. Its first use is the deterioration of Melbourne's road surfaces from one rating each.

## The Hodrick–Prescott Filter, Exact to Rounding

The filter now solves for the cycle first: its second differences give a system of the same size and conditioning, whose right-hand side is small, and the trend is the series less the cycle. The series is exactly its trend plus its cycle, the cycle sums to zero and is orthogonal to time to 2 × 10⁻¹⁶ (they missed by 6 × 10⁻¹² and 4 × 10⁻¹¹), and the trend of Lütkepohl's income series agrees with its exact rational solution to 3 × 10⁻¹⁵. The check that held it to statsmodels to 10⁻¹² is now held to that exact solution (`time_series/tests/make_hp_fixtures.py`), since statsmodels itself, solving for the trend directly, is off by 1.5 × 10⁻¹². All 654 checks pass.
