# 2026-11-15

## Graphical Models and Belief Functions; Decisions in One Step; Mixed and Latent-Variable Models

- **Bayesian networks** over discrete variables can be built, checked with reasons, and queried exactly by variable elimination ([decision 0023](../decisions/0023-graphical-models-and-belief-functions.md)): marginals, likelihood ratios, the most probable explanation, belief propagation, and EM from incomplete records. **Belief functions** after Dempster and Shafer hold evidence on sets of answers, with their rules of combination, discounting and the pignistic law.
- **Decisions in one step** ([decision 0024](../decisions/0024-decisions-in-one-step-and-the-value-of-information.md)):
  - under risk, ignorance and ambiguity, with credal sets and E-admissibility;
  - decision trees folded back;
  - the value of perfect and sample information;
  - stochastic dominance.
- **Linear mixed models** ([decision 0025](../decisions/0025-mixed-and-latent-variable-models.md)): random intercepts and slopes with an unstructured covariance, by profiled REML or ML, with BLUPs.
- **Latent variables** (also decision 0025): factor analysis by maximum likelihood with varimax, and latent class analysis. These are for the quantitative analysis beneath policy making.
