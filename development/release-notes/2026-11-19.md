# Release Notes: 19 November 2026

## Ideal Points from Votes

`latent` can now place the members of a body on a line from their recorded votes ([decision 0030](../decisions/0030-ideal-points-from-votes.md)). The model is the probit model of roll-call voting, fitted by the EM algorithm of Imai, Lo and Olmsted. Members who did not vote are missing, not counted against anything.

- **Fast and sure.** Plain EM creeps along the line's scale for a long time. The fit is now accelerated by SQUAREM, which keeps EM's monotone climb and stops when the parameters no longer move. The mode it reaches is the one a quasi-Newton search finds from the same start.
- **Intervals.** A parametric bootstrap refits the model on votes drawn from the fit. Each replicate is put on the fit's location and scale before its spread is read, so that an interval measures where a member stands, not the line's arbitrary units.
- **Checked** on simulated bodies with known positions: members are recovered in order, intervals narrow with more votes and cover the truth, a lopsided vote carries little information, and a member who never voted is said to be unplaced.
