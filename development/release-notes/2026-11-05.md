# 2026-11-05

## Hidden Markov Models, and HSVI's Interval Traced

In this update tyche reads a hidden state from what it emits ([decision 0011](../decisions/0011-hidden-markov-models.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0074](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0074-perception-and-decision-in-the-economy-atlas.md)).

- **Hidden Markov models.** `processes` now holds chains seen only through Gaussian observations. It gives the law of the state from the observations so far (the filter), from the whole sequence (the smoother), and the most probable path, with missing observations allowed. It fits the model to many sequences at once by expectation–maximisation, optionally with one deviation for every state, Hamilton's model of the business cycle. The likelihood and every smoothed law agree with the sum over all 729 paths of a small model to 10⁻¹².
- **Its first use.** The economics domain reads the regime of 212 economies, contraction, normal growth or a boom, from the World Bank's growth of real GDP; a policymaker's POMDP acts on that belief.
- **HSVI's interval, traced.** The solver now records its lower and upper bounds before each trial, so that a page can draw the interval closing on the optimal value.
