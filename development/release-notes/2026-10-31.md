# Release Notes: 31 October 2026

## 1. Spectral Inference, Harmonic Analysis, and Point Patterns

In this update tyche reads rhythms and places as a tidal analyst and a spatial statistician do ([decision 0005](../decisions/0005-spectral-inference-harmonics-and-point-patterns.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0060](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0060-streets-as-open-systems.md)).

- **Spectral inference.** Intervals for spectral estimates, Welch's degrees of freedom, the levels of coherence and bicoherence, red-noise backgrounds and their surrogates, and aliasing, held to SciPy's χ² quantiles.
- **Harmonic analysis**, the method of tidal analysis: amplitudes and phases at given frequencies for any spacing of times and any gaps, Rayleigh's criterion, and errors white or from the residual's spectrum. The urban-science domain predicts every Melbourne street's tide with it.
- **Lomb–Scargle** periodograms of uneven series, with their false-alarm levels.
- **Point patterns of marks.** The co-location quotient against permutation nulls, free or stratified, and Clark and Evans's ratio with Donnelly's correction.
