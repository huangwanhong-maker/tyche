# 2026-11-12

## Information Through a Record

tyche can now say how much of the difference between two laws a record shows, and how much it hides ([decision 0016](../decisions/0016-information-through-a-record.md)).

- **What a record shows.** Two laws on the same outcomes differ by their relative entropy. Seen only through a record (a coarsening, a noisy reading, any kernel from outcomes to records), that difference splits exactly into a part the record shows and a part it hides, record by record. A record that shows the outcome hides nothing; one that shows nothing hides everything; a record garbled further never shows more.
- **Two mechanisms, one record.** The same split says when two candidate mechanisms cannot be told apart from what was recorded, and what a second way of recording would add.
- **Processes that part.** For two jump processes on one set of states, the rate at which their paths part is given at any law and at the first one's stationary law, in closed form for two states and checked against matrix exponentials over a short time.

It is held to 22 checks: the chain rule to rounding on 200 random laws and kernels, the limits, and the closed forms.
