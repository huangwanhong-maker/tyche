# 2026-11-07

## Spatial Autocorrelation

In this update tyche says whether values at neighbouring places resemble each other ([decision 0013](../decisions/0013-spatial-autocorrelation.md)).

- **Moran's I** over the whole map, with its expectation, both of Cliff and Ord's variances, z-scores, p-values and a permutation test.
- **Local Moran's I** for each place, with its kind of association (high among high, low among low, and the outliers) and a p-value by conditional permutation.
- **Neighbours** given by hand or found as each point's k nearest, in the plane or on the sphere, where they are exact across the antimeridian too.

Every statistic and every place's value agrees with PySAL's esda on symmetric and unsymmetric weights, in 44 checks. The Urban Science Workbench uses it to test a regression's residuals wherever its rows are places.

## Annual Losses and Exceedance Curves

In this update tyche says what a record of losses says of the year ([decision 0012](../decisions/0012-exceedance.md)): the average annual loss of a catalogue of events, the loss to expect once in so many years, and the area under a published curve, with what is assumed beyond its points reported apart. Freetown's flood assessment is its first use: its annual loss recomputed from its events is its own figure to the cent, and its published curve is those events in order.
