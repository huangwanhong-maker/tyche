# Release Notes: 21 November 2026

## Forecasts Compared

`time_series` can now say whether one forecast is more accurate than another ([decision 0031](../decisions/0031-forecasts-compared.md)): rolling one-step forecasts from any forecaster, the three that need no model (the random walk, the historical mean and a fitted first-order autoregression), Newey and West's long-run variance on its own, and Diebold and Mariano's test of equal accuracy with the small-sample correction of Harvey, Leybourne and Newbold, which keeps its size at 5% in runs of fifty.
