# Release Notes: 1 November 2026

## 1. Change Points, Penalties, Factorisations, Fine-Mapping, Testing and Learning

In this update tyche gains the statistics that abnormal genomes and the prediction of expression need. Decisions [0006](../decisions/0006-change-points-penalties-and-factorisations.md), [0007](../decisions/0007-sum-of-single-effects-variance-components-and-multiple-testing.md) and [0008](../decisions/0008-the-learning-module.md) record them, for the infrastructure's [ADR 0064](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0064-abnormal-genomes-their-description-and-expression.md).

- **Change points.** A series can be cut where its mean, its variance or its Poisson rate changes:
  - by exact optimal partitioning, and by PELT, which gives the same answer with far fewer evaluations;
  - by binary and wild binary segmentation;
  - by circular binary segmentation with its permutation test.
  - Several series can be cut jointly; read depth and allele fractions are segmented this way.
- **Penalised regression.** Ridge, lasso and elastic nets along their paths, for continuous and binary outcomes, with cross-validation. They agree with scikit-learn to 10⁻¹².
- **Non-negative fits.** Non-negative least squares, and non-negative matrix factorisation. Its stability across resamplings chooses the number of factors, as SigProfiler chooses the number of mutational signatures. There is also a mixture of binomials for the clones of a tumour.
- **Fine-mapping and heritability.** SuSiE's sum of single effects fits individual data and summary statistics, reproducing susieR. Variance components are estimated by REML and by Haseman–Elston. A smoothing spline is added.
- **Testing**, a new module: Bonferroni, Holm and Benjamini–Hochberg; π₀ and q-values; permutation p-values with FastQTL's beta approximation.
- **Learning**, a new module: layers, losses, backpropagation and Adam, for small networks of sequences. Gradients are checked against finite differences, and training gives the same result on any number of threads. Ranks and rank correlations are added too.
- **A correction.** The Student t law's upper tail no longer loses its digits below 10⁻¹⁶. A GWAS's smallest p-values depend on it.
