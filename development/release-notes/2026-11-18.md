# Release Notes: 18 November 2026

## 1. Comparing Groups, and Testing What a Search Finds

`testing` can now compare groups and decide about hypotheses that arrive one at a time ([decision 0027](../decisions/0027-comparing-groups-and-testing-what-a-search-finds.md)).
- **Two groups, or a group and a level.** Weighted means with Kish's effective size; Welch's test; a share against a level by the score test; two shares by Newcombe's interval. Counts may be fractional. All are held to SciPy and statsmodels.
- **A bootstrap of clusters,** for how stable a result is when whole areas are resampled.
- **Online control of false discoveries.** LOND, LORD++, SAFFRON, α-investing and e-LOND decide each hypothesis as it arrives, never revising a decision; e-LOND holds under any dependence. A rule's state replays exactly from the values it was given.
- **E-values:** calibrators from p-values, their mean and product, and e-BH.
- **The reusable holdout.** Thresholdout lets a part of the data kept aside be consulted many times within a budget. In the check, a classifier of pure noise is rated 0.629 by plain reuse of the holdout and 0.516 by Thresholdout; the truth is 0.5.
- The spending sequence of Javanmard and Montanari now sums to 1. The constant they print, 0.07720838, makes it sum to 0.976.
