# 0017. Classes and Communities

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally (2026-11-12).
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decided by:** the infrastructure's ADR 0101, which founds its ecology domain for urban forests (the street trees of Melbourne and of New York's street-tree census, by species).

## 1. Context

The ecology domain will ask of a city's trees how many species there are, how evenly the trees are spread among them, how the diversity divides among blocks or districts, how much of the city's flora a census or a sample has seen, and whether the composition of plantings differs between places. None of this names an object in nature: the diversity of a law and the number of classes a sample has not seen are questions about classes and counts, asked equally of a vocabulary, a k-mer library, a canon of events or the kinds of business on a street. By the infrastructure's rule, a general method goes into a foundation before a domain uses it, so these belong in tyche. The dissimilarities, ordination and permutation tests of samples of counts are multivariate statistics, and belong in `multivariate`.

## 2. Decision

**A new module, `classes`** (namespace `tyche::classes`), placed after `descriptive` and using `probability` and calx's `linalg`, `random` and `special_functions`:

- **samples** of individuals (abundance: X_i, n, f_k) and of sampling units (incidence: T, Y_i, U, Q_k), counts given as `std::span<const double>`;
- **the diversity of a law:** Hill numbers of any order with their limits at q = 1 and ∞, Rényi entropies, profiles, and Leinster and Cobbold's similarity-sensitive diversity;
- **its partition among units:** Jost's multiplicative partition with equal weights for every q, with given unequal weights for q = 1 only (refused otherwise, as Jost proves alpha and beta are then not independent), and Chao, Chiu and Jost's size-weighted alpha for every q; Sørensen- and Jaccard-type overlaps;
- **estimates from a sample:** Chao1 in its classic and bias-corrected forms with the delta-method variance and the log-normal interval, ACE, the sample coverage, the detection ratio, Chao, Wang and Jost's estimator of entropy and the asymptotes of orders 0, 1 and 2;
- **rarefaction and extrapolation** of Hill numbers (interpolation of any order, extrapolation of orders 0, 1 and 2) and of coverage, the size at a given coverage, and curves with intervals from Chao et al.'s bootstrap assemblage on calx's seeded streams;
- **incidence data:** Chao2, coverage and the richness curve along the number of units, with its bootstrap.

**`multivariate` gains communities:** dissimilarities (Bray–Curtis, Jaccard, Sørensen, Hellinger, chord, Euclidean), principal coordinates with negative eigenvalues reported and Lingoes's or Cailliez's correction, PERMANOVA with random and exact permutation p-values, PERMDISP, and the Mantel test with random and exact p-values.

Where the sources leave a choice, the choice is stated in `docs/glossary.md` §5: the factor (n − 1)/n in Chao1, its form when f₂ = 0, the detection ratio and coverage when f₂ = 0, the definition of the rarefied Hill numbers from the expected frequency counts, the bootstrap's standard error and interval, and the conventions of the permutation tests.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **The ecology domain holding these methods.** Rejected by the infrastructure's rule of reuse: vocabularies, genomic libraries and canons need the same estimators.
- **`descriptive` or `testing` holding them.** The estimators of unseen classes are inference under a sampling model, not description, and the partition and the curves are a body of their own; a module of their own keeps them together.
- **Borrowing iNEXT or vegan.** Neither is C++; their formulas are written here from the papers, and iNEXT's and scikit-bio's forms are named where they differ.

## 4. Verification

`check_tyche_diversity` (43), `check_tyche_richness` (50), `check_tyche_rarefaction` (55) and `check_tyche_incidence` (22) in `classes`, and `check_tyche_communities` (46) in `multivariate`, hold the methods to closed forms (Hill numbers of uniform and dyadic laws, beta of identical and disjoint units, Chao's variance, the overlaps as Sørensen's, Jaccard's and Morisita and Horn's indices, the ANOVA F), to identities (Leinster and Cobbold's with Z = I, C_qN = U_{(2−q)N}, the identity under the entropy estimator), to brute force (the rarefied Hill numbers as the means over every subsample, the incidence curve over every set of units, the exact unbiasedness of Ŝ_m and Ĉ_m over every multinomial sample, PERMANOVA's and Mantel's exact p-values over every relabelling), and to seeded simulations (the estimators approaching the truth, the bootstrap intervals covering it). scikit-bio 0.7.4's Hill numbers, Rényi entropies, ACE, Chao1 and Good's coverage are cross-checks, generated by a committed script.

tyche's checks rise from 843 to 1059.

## 5. Consequences

The ecology domain can be written against `tyche::classes` and `tyche::multivariate` without numerical code of its own. Left for later, and named in the modules' open questions: incidence-based diversity of orders 1 and 2, Hill numbers of any order estimated from samples, ICE and the variance of ACE, phylogenetic and functional diversity estimated from samples, shared richness between samples, PERMANOVA of several factors, distance-based redundancy analysis, non-metric scaling and the partial Mantel test.
