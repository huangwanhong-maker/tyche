# 0018. Compositions

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally (2026-11-13).
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** the infrastructure's ADR 0104, which founds its sociology domain with time use (how a day is divided among activities) and relations, and its ADR 0105, which founds a bridge domain of social provision (households and care).

## 1. Context

The sociology domain will ask how the 1,440 minutes of a day are divided among sleep, paid and unpaid work, care, leisure and travel, how that division differs between persons and changes over a life, and what moving half an hour from one activity to another does to an outcome; the domain of social provision will ask the same of a household's time and spending. Such data are compositions: positive parts of a fixed whole, whose information lies in their ratios. Ordinary statistics of the parts mislead (the closure forces negative covariances, and a part's change is read against a total that moves with it), and the remedy, Aitchison's geometry of the simplex with its log-ratio coordinates, names no object in nature: the same methods serve the minerals of a rock, the shares of a budget, an electorate or a microbiome read as proportions. By the infrastructure's rule a general method goes into a foundation before a domain uses it, so they belong in tyche. The linear models they need are tyche's least squares, used and not written again.

## 2. Decision

**A new module, `compositions`** (namespace `tyche::compositions`), using `regression` and `probability` and calx's `linalg`:

- **the simplex and its geometry:** closure to any total, perturbation, powering, the Aitchison inner product, norm and distance, clr and alr with their inverses;
- **orthonormal coordinates:** bases built from any sequential binary partition, validated (Egozcue and Pawlowsky-Glahn 2005), pivot coordinates as the default (Hron, Filzmoser and Thompson 2012), ilr and its inverse, balances, changes of basis;
- **a sample described:** the centre (closed geometric mean), the variation matrix, the clr covariance, the total variance and the metric standard deviation;
- **zeros:** multiplicative replacement of rounded zeros with a stated δ or a fraction (0.65 by default) of detection limits, keeping every ratio among the non-zero parts (Martín-Fernández, Barceló-Vidal and Pawlowsky-Glahn 2003); essential zeros refused with the reason;
- **compositions in linear models:** a composition as predictor through ilr coordinates in `regression::ols` (any of its covariances), with clr coefficients, pivot effects of each part by rotation, and reallocations (compositional isotemporal substitution, one for one and one for remaining; Dumuid et al. 2018, 2019) with standard errors and intervals; a composition as response, coordinate by coordinate, with effects as compositions and predictions carried back.

Where the sources leave a choice, the choice is stated in `docs/glossary.md` §6: closure to a stated total; the divisor n − ddof of the variation matrix (ddof = 1, tyche's convention, where Pawlowsky-Glahn et al. write n); the orientation of a balance (positive when the +1 parts are larger); pivot coordinates with the parts in order as the default basis; the reference of a reallocation in its own units.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **The sociology domain holding these methods.** Rejected by the infrastructure's rule of reuse: geology, economics and the sciences of life read compositions alike.
- **`multivariate` holding them.** Compositional data analysis has its own sample space and geometry, to which every multivariate method is carried by coordinates; a module of its own keeps that geometry in one place, and `multivariate`'s methods can be applied to its coordinates.
- **Borrowing R's `compositions` or scikit-bio.** Neither is C++; their formulas are written here from the papers, and scikit-bio is a cross-check generated by a committed script.
- **The additive log-ratio as the default coordinates.** Rejected: alr is not an isometry, so distances and least squares in it depend on the denominator; it is offered only for exchange.

## 4. Verification

`check_tyche_simplex` (55), `check_tyche_composition_sample` (43) and `check_tyche_compositional_regression` (40) hold the module to identities (the vector-space axioms of perturbation and powering, the two forms of the inner product, the isometry and linearity of ilr, the orthonormality of 95 bases, the total variance in four forms), closed forms (pivot coordinates as Hron et al.'s formula, two-part models, reallocations as Σ b_i ln(x¹_i/x⁰_i)), exact properties (the ratios of non-zero parts kept by replacement, the reverse reallocation exactly opposite, fitted values independent of the basis, pivot effects equal to refitted models) and seeded simulations (logistic-normal centres and variances, known coefficients recovered). scikit-bio 0.7.4's closure, clr, powering, perturbation, inner product, `sbp_basis` coordinates, `pairwise_vlr`, `centralize` and `multi_replace` are cross-checks, generated by a committed script.

## 5. Consequences

The sociology and social-provision domains can describe and model the division of a day or a budget against `tyche::compositions` without numerical code of their own. Left for later, and named in the module's open questions: model-based replacement of zeros and the modelling of essential zeros, multivariate tests for a composition as response, robust estimation and compositional principal components, the Dirichlet and logistic-normal laws as distributions, and compositions whose total is itself informative.
